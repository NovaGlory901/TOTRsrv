Schedule

Finds music that is legally free to download (Creative Commons or public domain, from the release's own license record). Nothing is downloaded until you press Add to library.

Free-to-stream is not the same as free-to-download, so releases without a license record are never suggested. Keep the credit lines if you share anything.

Genres to search

Tick the genres you want searched. Add new ones any time: they are remembered, even while switched off.

Where and when

Every release gets a quality score. It rewards real albums, netlabel releases, downloads and reviews at the source, and artists MusicBrainz knows; it marks down stock, "royalty free" and background-music uploads. Anything below the minimum is not suggested (0 suggests everything). Things on your wanted list are always shown.

Wanted

Artists and albums you are missing. Every search also looks for these. The Maintenance tab can fill this list for you.
